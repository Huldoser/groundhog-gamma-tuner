Global Settings

Steps

How far each step moves, how often the tuner checks, and how long it holds at the ceiling before saving the setpoint. Monitor interval is at least 1 second. Tune interval is at least 60 seconds.

Temperature

The ASIC cap is copied onto a miner when it is added. Tolerances apply to every miner.

Flatline

Restart a miner whose live hashrate and one-minute average both stay on the same reading for longer than a minute. A steady average alone is not a hang.

AutoTuner Settings

Limits for the selected miner.

Frequency

Min is 350–1100 MHz. 350 is the lowest BM1370 preset in AxeOS v2.15.1.

Voltage

Min stays 1000–1400 mV. 1000 is the lowest BM1370 voltage preset. At the frequency ceiling the tuner trims voltage down, then restores it if hashrate or errors get worse. Voltage is lowered again only after frequency is already at its minimum.
